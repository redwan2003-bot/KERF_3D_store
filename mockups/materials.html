DESIGN FOR PRINT / A1 GUIDANCE

Build for
the layer.

Our quoted tolerances are practical production targets, not promises of CNC machining. We review overhangs, wall thickness, bridges, and part orientation before printing.

01DIMENSIONSKeep each part inside the 256 mm cube. Larger models may require a split-and-join plan.
02WALLSWe recommend at least 1.2 mm for general PLA forms and thicker walls for functional loads.
03TOLERANCESAllow approximately 0.2–0.4 mm clearance for fitted parts; final results depend on geometry and orientation.
04HEATPLA and TPU are not intended for sustained high-heat environments. Ask before selecting PETG for a demanding application.
